# Case studies

Incident write-ups that test the frameworks and checklists in this hub against what actually happened. Each follows the same shape: summary, timeline, what went wrong, why it matters for privacy/cyber/compliance programmes, framework mapping, sources.

| Case | Date | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| [Binance hack](binance-2026-hack.md) | 29 Oct 2026 | Largest crypto theft on record (~US$6.3bn); endpoint compromise of signing infrastructure; issuer freezes; DPRK attribution |
| [OpenAI / Hugging Face incident](openai-hugging-face-2026.md) | Jul 2026 | First documented autonomous AI intrusion; sandbox escape; multi-agent coordination; agent security governance |
| [EFS blood-plasma freezers (France)](efs-coolworld-refrigeration-2026.md) | 1–2 Nov 2026 | OT/ICS integrity attack via a cooling contractor; cyber-physical patient-safety risk; NIS2 supply-chain and reporting; GDPR on donor samples |
| [United States v. Sullivan (Uber CSO)](uber-sullivan-ciso-obstruction.md) | 2016 breach; conviction 2022; affirmed Mar 2025 | Personal criminal liability for breach concealment; bug bounty and NDAs |
| [SEC v. SolarWinds and its CISO](solarwinds-sec-ciso.md) | Oct 2023 – Nov 2025 | Cyber disclosure and "security statement" liability; CISO as defendant |
| [Cyber risk in M&A: Marriott/Starwood, Verizon/Yahoo](cyber-risk-in-m-and-a-marriott-yahoo.md) | 2016–2020 | Inherited breaches, ICO fine, US$350m price cut and liability split |
| [Breach class actions: Equifax, Capital One, Optus, Medibank](data-breach-class-actions-equifax-capital-one-optus-medibank.md) | 2017–2026 | Settlements, regulator penalties and live Australian proceedings on "reasonable security" |
| [Meta €1.2bn transfers fine](meta-1-2bn-data-transfers-2023.md) | May 2023 | SCCs and supplementary measures; EDPB binding decision |
| [Operation Snowball](operation-snowball-ai-agent-2026.md) | Reported 8 Nov 2026 | Jailbroken AI agent attack on a telecom and 13 hosted customers |
