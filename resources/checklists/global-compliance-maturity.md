**Source:** [IAPP, 10 Tips for Global Compliance](../../analyses/iapp-global-compliance-tips.md) (2024). Score each item 0–3 (0 = absent, 1 = ad hoc, 2 = defined, 3 = measured and improving). Prioritise the lowest scores.

## Using the result
- Scores ≤ 1 on items 1, 4 or 6 indicate the programme lacks a design; fix these first.
- Scores ≤ 1 on items 2 or 3 indicate blind spots to regulatory change; see the technical connectors in [`../../connectors/external-hubs.md`](../../connectors/external-hubs.md).
- Re-score annually or after a major regulatory event.
