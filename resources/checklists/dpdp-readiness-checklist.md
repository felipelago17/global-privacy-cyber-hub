# India DPDP readiness checklist

**Source:** [DPDP in Practice](../../analyses/india-dpdp-practice.md) (Novation Strategy, Oct 2026). Target date for operational readiness: **13 May 2027** (verify against official notifications).

## 1. Applicability and inventory
- [ ] Confirm applicability (processing of digital personal data in India, or outside India in connection with offering goods/services in India)
- [ ] Data inventory of India-related personal data, systems and vendors
- [ ] Determine whether you may be notified as a **Significant Data Fiduciary**

## 2. Lawful basis
- [ ] Every processing activity mapped to **consent** or a listed **legitimate use** — no reliance on GDPR-style legitimate interest
- [ ] Activities with no available basis stopped or redesigned
- [ ] Employment-related processing mapped to the employment legitimate use

## 3. Consent and notice
- [ ] Notices in plain language; available in the scheduled languages where required
- [ ] Consent captured by clear affirmative action, specific to purpose, unbundled
- [ ] Withdrawal as easy as giving consent; downstream processors informed on withdrawal
- [ ] Consent Manager interactions assessed, if applicable

## 4. Children
- [ ] Age-assurance approach decided; verifiable parental consent mechanism built
- [ ] No tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children

## 5. Vendors and processors
- [ ] Contracts with processors include breach notification SLAs shorter than statutory timelines, audit rights, sub-processor controls
- [ ] Fiduciary-level accountability acknowledged in governance (responsibility cannot be outsourced)

## 6. Breach management
- [ ] Incident classification distinguishes "personal data breach" from security events — **every breach is reportable**, no threshold
- [ ] Notification templates to the Data Protection Board and to Data Principals pre-approved
- [ ] Timelines and escalation path tested in a tabletop exercise

## 7. Rights and grievances
- [ ] Access, correction, erasure, nomination and grievance processes operational
- [ ] Grievance mechanism published with response timelines

## 8. Transfers
- [ ] Monitor government notifications restricting transfers to specific countries/territories
- [ ] Sector regulators' localisation rules (e.g. RBI, IRDAI, SEBI) checked alongside DPDP

## 9. Significant Data Fiduciary (if notified)
- [ ] DPO based in India appointed
- [ ] Independent data auditor engaged; periodic DPIAs scheduled
- [ ] Algorithmic due diligence process documented

## 10. Governance and evidence
- [ ] Board/management briefed on penalty exposure
- [ ] Policies, training and records in place by the readiness date
