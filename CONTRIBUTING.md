# Contributing

Thank you for helping build this hub. The aim is a **curated**, not exhaustive, collection: fewer, better-sourced entries beat long unvetted lists.

## What to contribute

- **Analyses** (`analyses/`): a focused write-up of one paper, opinion, ruling or guidance document.
- **Paper links** (`resources/papers/links.md`): a link to a primary source with a two-to-three sentence abstract.
- **Framework notes** (`resources/frameworks/`): structured notes on a law, standard or regulatory framework.
- **Checklists and templates** (`resources/checklists/`, `resources/tools/`): practical, reusable material.
- **Connectors** (`connectors/`): external hubs, tools or integration ideas, with a line on why they matter.
- **Related repositories** (`related-forks.md`): awesome lists and projects worth referencing.

## Standards

1. **Cite the primary source.** Every analysis must link to the original document (or a public version) and state its date and author/organisation.
2. **Summarise, don't reproduce.** Write in your own words. Keep quotations short and attributed. Do not upload copyrighted PDFs to the repo; link to them instead.
3. **Use the analysis template.** Each analysis should contain: *Source*, *Summary*, *Key findings*, *Practical implications*, *Open questions / critique*, *Source notes*.
4. **Mark dates and status.** Regulatory positions change. State "as of <month year>" where relevant and flag anything that is a proposal rather than adopted law.
5. **No legal advice.** Keep the educational/reference framing. Do not add client-specific or confidential material.
6. **Jurisdiction and industry tags.** Add a short line at the top of each analysis listing the jurisdictions and sectors it touches (e.g. `EU · Healthcare · AI Act`).

## Process

1. Open an issue describing the source you want to add and why it belongs here.
2. Fork the repository and create a branch (`analysis/<short-name>` or `connector/<short-name>`).
3. Add your file(s) following the structure above; update `README.md` tables if you add an analysis.
4. Open a pull request. Keep PRs focused on one source or one connector set.

## Style

- Markdown, sentence-case headings, British or American English consistently within a file.
- Prefer tables and short bullets for practitioner material; prose for critique.
- File names: lowercase, hyphen-separated (`edpb-ai-models.md`).
