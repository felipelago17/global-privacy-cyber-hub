Projects worth starring, forking or referencing. This mirrors the "awesome list" convention and the curated approach of the IAPP, OneTrust and OECD collections. The policy here is **reference first, fork second**: fork only where we intend to maintain a focused subset or contribute upstream.

## Suggested contribution strategy

1. **Upstream first.** Where a resource belongs in `awesome-data-privacy` or `awesome-compliance`, open a PR there and link back.
2. **Local curated subset.** Maintain here only what is specific to this hub's focus: AI governance, cloud DPIAs, agent security, PETs and multi-jurisdictional compliance (EU, UK, US, India, GCC).
3. **Star, then evaluate.** Star candidate repos; add them here only after checking they are maintained and accurately described.
