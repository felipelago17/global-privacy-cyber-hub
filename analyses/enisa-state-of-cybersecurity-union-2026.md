# ENISA 2026 Report on the State of Cybersecurity in the Union

`ENISA · NIS2 Art. 18 · EU Cybersecurity Index · Threat landscape · Strategic compass · Published 10 Nov 2026`

Status as of 14 Nov 2026. I could not open the report PDF (the ENISA site blocks automated retrieval), so this page rests on ENISA's publication page, search snippets and secondary coverage. Figures should be checked against the report before they are quoted.

## What it is

The second edition of ENISA's biennial report, adopted with the European Commission and the NIS Cooperation Group and published on 10 Nov 2026. The first edition appeared on 3 Dec 2024. The 2026 edition describes how the topics from 2024 have evolved, adds new elements and introduces a "strategic compass" that sets out the main directions of the EU cybersecurity ecosystem together with policy recommendations. The EU Cybersecurity Index is developed under NIS2 Art. 18(3) to assess the maturity of Member States' capabilities and resources.

## Headline findings (as reported)

| Topic | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Threat level | Rated "substantial", unchanged from the previous report | Agence Europe |
| Maturity | EU average index 71.53 out of 100, judged satisfactory; more than 80% of Member States rated "mature", two "advanced", three "established" | Agence Europe; Industrial Cyber |
| Attack types | DDoS is the most common type, at 51.3% of incidents | Agence Europe |
| Motives | Ideologically motivated attacks are the largest share by volume (57.3% of 2025 incidents) but with limited impact | Agence Europe |
| Targets | Public administrations are the most targeted sector (31.8%), well ahead of business services (8.5%) and transport (8%) | Agence Europe |
| Geopolitics | The war in Ukraine and conflicts in the Middle East continue to shape cyber activity against the EU | Agence Europe |
| AI | AI is seen as an amplifier of conventional attacks and as speeding up vulnerability discovery; ENISA has not yet built this into its threat assessment and says the question is under review | Agence Europe |
| NIS2 | Several Member States have still not transposed NIS2 | Agence Europe |
| Skills | Countries including cybersecurity in secondary-school curricula fell from 17 to 13 between 2024 and 2026 | Secondary coverage |

Recommendations reported include working with the NIS Cooperation Group to prioritise SMEs covered by NIS2, closing gaps in crisis management, and investing in national CSIRTs over the next two years. Coverage also reports the report addresses EU-level risks and dependencies across technologies, products and supply chains.

## Why it matters for privacy and cyber teams

1. **Benchmark for board reporting.** An EU average maturity of 71.53 and a "substantial" threat rating give a neutral reference point, though the index measures Member States, not individual organisations.
2. **Where the volume is versus where the harm is.** DDoS and ideologically motivated activity dominate counts, while impact is concentrated elsewhere. Incident statistics by volume should not set priorities on their own.
3. **AI is flagged but not yet quantified.** ENISA's own caution is a useful counterweight to vendor claims about AI-driven attack growth.
4. **NIS2 implementation is uneven.** Entities operating across Member States face different transposition dates and reporting formats; see the [Luxembourg ILR reporting update](nis2-luxembourg-ilr-security-measures-reporting-2027.md).
5. **Resilience beyond attacks.** The cloud outage commentary in [cloud concentration](cloud-centrality-menace-technollama-2026.md) fits the report's emphasis on dependencies and crisis management.

## What to check in the full report

- The methodology and weighting of the EU Cybersecurity Index, and the ranking of individual Member States.
- Sector breakdowns, ransomware and supply-chain figures, which I could not confirm.
- The exact wording of the AI findings and the policy recommendations in the strategic compass.
- Whether the 57.3% and 51.3% figures refer to incidents analysed in ENISA's separate 2026 Threat Landscape report.
