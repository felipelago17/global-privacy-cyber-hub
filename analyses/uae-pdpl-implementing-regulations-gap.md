# UAE PDPL — the missing Executive Regulations and the new AI and Data Authority

`UAE · PDPL · Federal Decree-Law 45/2021 · Executive Regulations · Regulator · Source reliability`

## Source

- **Instruments:** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 concerning the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL); announcement of a Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data (14 June 2026)
- **Type:** Status note. The PDPL text was checked on the official legislation portal; the status of the Executive Regulations rests on law-firm commentary
- **Links:** see Sources

## Summary

The PDPL has been in force since 2 January 2022, but the Executive Regulations it depends on have not been issued, according to law-firm commentary from June and July 2026. Several commercial websites say the opposite. Until an implementing text appears on the official portal, onshore compliance programmes should build to the Decree-Law itself and treat any quoted procedural detail (notification deadlines, fine amounts, transfer mechanisms) as unverified.

## Key points

### What the Decree-Law itself settles
- **Scope and exclusions (Art. 2).** The exclusions include personal health data and personal banking and credit data that have their own legislation, and companies and establishments in free zones that have their own data-protection legislation. The free-zone exclusion is not limited to the financial free zones.
- **Consent as the default (Art. 4).** Processing without the data subject's consent is prohibited, subject to the listed cases.

### What is still open
- Law-firm commentary states that the implementing regulations, needed to operationalise lawful bases, data-subject rights, cross-border transfers and breach notification, have not been issued (Morgan Lewis, 15 June 2026).
- The UAE Data Office, the regulator named for the PDPL, is described as never having become fully operational (Morgan Lewis; Ashurst, July 2026).

### The institutional change
- On 14 June 2026 a Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data was announced, consolidating the UAE Artificial Intelligence Office, the digital-government sector of the TDRA and the UAE Data Office.
- The market expectation reported by law firms is that the Authority will finalise the regulations and bring enforcement of the PDPL within its mandate.

### The source-reliability problem
- Commercial guides describe "2026 Executive Regulations" with compliance windows and deadlines, and at least one commentator notes that contradictory Cabinet Decision numbers are in circulation.
- None of these claims could be matched to a text on the official portal when this note was prepared.

## Implications for compliance programmes

| Area | Action |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Build to the Decree-Law: records, lawful basis, security, processor terms, transfer mapping |
| Breach response | Do not assume a fixed onshore notification clock; use the stricter free-zone clocks as a working standard where a group spans regimes |
| AI projects | For fine-tuning on personal data, document purpose and lawful basis for the training use itself; do not rely on consent given for another purpose |
| Monitoring | Check the official portal and the new Authority's publications, not secondary sites |
| Vendor claims | Ask any vendor citing "PDPL Executive Regulations" for the instrument number and a portal link |

## Caveats

- Status as at 13 November 2026. This will date quickly once the Authority starts issuing instruments.
- The absence of a text on the portal was observed by search, not by an exhaustive review of the Official Gazette.
